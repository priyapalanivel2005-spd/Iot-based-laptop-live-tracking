# Future Scope

The IoT-Based Laptop Live Tracking & Anti-Theft System can be enhanced further with the following future improvements:


- 📱 **Mobile Application** – Develop an Android and iOS app for tracking and alerts on smartphones.
- 📡 **GSM-Based Tracking** – Enable tracking when Wi-Fi connectivity is unavailable by adding GSM communication.
- 📷 **Camera Integration** – Add a camera feature to support thief identification.
- 🤖 **AI-Based Movement Detection** – Use AI to analyze movement and improve suspicious-activity detection.
- 🚨 **Tamper Detection Alarm** – Add an alarm to alert the user when the tracking device is tampered with.
